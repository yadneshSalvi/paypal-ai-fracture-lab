# Saffron: two partial-refund requests compete for one balance

Handle `{refundIntentId,accountId,captureId,amountMinor,attemptId}`. The immutable `refund_authorizations[refundIntentId]` contains approved, accountId, intentId, captureId, amountMinor and currency. The corresponding intents row supplies the original captured amount. Enforce scope and exact authorized payload before effects; a changed amount or capture returns conflict/409. Read and verify the bound capture's identity, currency and original amount. Independent refund intents are legitimate, but they compete for the same captured balance.

Atomically reserve capacity in the target before dispatching a refund. Pending, unknown and completed local refunds consume capacity; explicit provider refusals and proven pre-dispatch failures may release it. For this fixture there are no external refunds. A caller must be denied locally with capacity/409 when reservations exhaust the balance, rather than relying on PayPal to reject an excessive write. Duplicate concurrent callers for one refundIntentId must share one operation, preserve the exact authorized payload and use the provider request-ID field. Different attempt IDs are irrelevant to identity.

Record completed results once in `refund_results[refundIntentId]` with refundId, captureId and amountMinor. Return completed/200 only on a matching COMPLETED refund; pending/202 or unknown/202 otherwise. A lost response without a refund ID must stay unknown and reserved, without another dispatch. A known refund ID may be reread. Authentication/encoding before dispatch is not_sent/503; known refusal is refused/422; foreign account forbidden/403. Keep unexpected store/programming failures visible. All amounts are positive integer minor units.
