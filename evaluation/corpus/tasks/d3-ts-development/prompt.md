# Beacon: duplicate capture notifications are one entitlement

Handle `{accountId,event}`. The envelope has id, event_type and resource.id. First verify the exact envelope with context.verify; a `verified` field in the payload is not trustworthy. For PAYMENT.CAPTURE.COMPLETED, `capture_bindings[resource.id]` maps to intentId and `intents[intentId]` contains accountId, authorized, amountMinor and currency. Never infer ownership or payment from event.custom_id, resource.status or its amount alone. Read the mapped capture with the SDK.

Grant exactly one `entitlements[intentId]` row (intentId, captureId, amountMinor) only for the expected COMPLETED capture with matching custom identity, amount and currency. A pending/refunded or unrelated capture must not grant access. Verified events can be duplicated, concurrently delivered or arrive out of order. A read/store outage must not permanently consume an event before its required effect. Record processed envelope identities durably; reusing a processed event ID with a different authenticated envelope is conflict/409. Identical successful duplicates are accepted/200. Invalid signature is invalid/401, foreign account forbidden/403, irrelevant event ignored/200, and an unavailable required read unknown/503 so delivery can retry. No payment writes are permitted. Unexpected programming/store errors must remain visible.
